How to Measure

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal.

Length

Measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the hem.

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, keeping the tape comfortably loose.

Foot Length

Stand on a piece of paper and mark the heel and longest toe, then measure the distance.

Tips

  • - If you're between sizes, we recommend ordering the larger size for a more comfortable fit during play.
  • - Our jerseys have a regular athletic fit - not too tight, not too loose.
  • - For socks, choose based on your EU shoe size for the best fit.
